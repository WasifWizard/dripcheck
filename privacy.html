Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 28, 2026  ·  Last Updated: February 28, 2026

01

Introduction

DripCheck ("we," "our," or "us") operates the DripCheck mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our app.

By using DripCheck, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

02

Information We Collect

Information You Provide:

  • Account Information: Email address, password (encrypted), username
  • Profile Data: Name, preferences, style settings
  • Wardrobe Content: Photos of clothing items you upload
  • Outfit Photos: Photos you submit for AI rating
  • Usage Data: App interactions, features used, ratings given

Automatically Collected:

  • Device Information: Device type, OS version, app version
  • Analytics: Screen views, feature usage (via Mixpanel)
  • Error Reports: Crash logs, error messages (via Sentry)
03

How We Use Your Information

  • Provide Services: AI outfit analysis, wardrobe organization, outfit generation
  • Improve Experience: Personalize recommendations based on your style
  • Communication: Send important updates, respond to support requests
  • Analytics: Understand usage patterns to improve the app
  • Legal Compliance: Comply with applicable laws and regulations
04

AI and Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate DripCheck:

Google Gemini AI Analyzes outfit photos and wardrobe items. Images are processed securely and are not stored by Google.
Supabase Secure cloud storage for your data, encrypted at rest.
Mixpanel Anonymous analytics only. No personal photos are shared.
Sentry Error monitoring and crash reporting. No personal data is shared.
05

Data Storage and Security

  • Encryption: All data encrypted in transit (SSL/TLS) and at rest
  • Location: Data stored on secure servers via Supabase infrastructure
  • Access: Only you can access your wardrobe and ratings
  • Retention: Data stored until you delete your account
06

Data Sharing

We Never

  • Sell your personal information
  • Share your photos with advertisers
  • Use your data for unrelated purposes

We May Share

  • With service providers under strict confidentiality
  • If required by law or court order
  • To protect rights and prevent fraud
07

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your data
  • Delete: Permanently delete your account by emailing privacy@trydripcheck.com
  • Correct: Update inaccurate information in-app or via support
  • Export: Download your wardrobe data
  • Opt-out: Disable analytics in app settings

For GDPR or CCPA requests, email privacy@trydripcheck.com

08

Children's Privacy

DripCheck is not intended for users under 13 years old. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If we learn we have collected data from a child under 13, we will delete it immediately.

09

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of DripCheck after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
