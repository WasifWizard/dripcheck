Quick Answers

How do I delete my account? +
Go to Settings — Profile — Delete Account inside the app, or email support@trydripcheck.com with subject "Delete Account". All data is permanently removed within 30 days.
How do I request a copy of my data? +
Email privacy@trydripcheck.com with the subject "Data Export Request". We'll process your request within 30 days in accordance with applicable privacy laws.
I found a bug. How do I report it? +
Email support@trydripcheck.com with a description of the issue, your device model, OS version, and steps to reproduce it. We'll investigate and respond within 24–48 hours.
Where can I find the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service? +
Both are available on this website: Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. They're also linked in the app under Settings.
